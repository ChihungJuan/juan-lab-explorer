The brain, measured and moved量測大腦，也改變大腦

For 26 years this lab has asked two questions: How does the brain choose what to attend to, hold in mind, and stop itself from doing? And — once we know — can we reach in and change it? Don't take our word for it. Run the experiments yourself or explore our 140+ published papers. 二十六年來，本實驗室追問兩個問題：大腦如何決定要注意什麼、記住什麼、以及何時該踩煞車？而當我們知道了答案，能不能真的介入並改變它？親自跑一次實驗，或探索我們 140 篇發表論文。

Research Lines研究主題

Six lines of work, one question underneath六條研究主線，底下是同一個問題

Everything here comes back to the frontoparietal control system — the network that decides what gets attention, what gets remembered, and what gets stopped. We map it, then we try to move it. 所有研究都回到同一個系統：額頂葉控制網路——決定什麼被注意、什麼被記住、什麼被抑制。我們先把它畫出來，再設法改變它。

    Training訓練內容

    What you would actually learn here你在這裡會真正學到的東西

    Not a reading list — a set of hands-on skills. Few labs anywhere let one student touch all of these. 這不是一份閱讀清單，而是一套實作技能。很少有實驗室能讓同一位學生接觸到全部這些方法。

    Who we're looking for我們在找什麼樣的人

    Curiosity matters more than background. Students here have arrived from psychology, life science, electrical engineering, medicine, physics and sports science — and the mix is the point, because the questions need all of it. 好奇心比背景更重要。本實驗室的成員來自心理學、生命科學、電機、醫學、物理與運動科學——這種混合正是關鍵，因為我們的問題需要所有這些視角。

    • You like puzzles that have an answer. Every question here is settled by an experiment, not by argument. 你喜歡有答案的謎題。這裡的每個問題都靠實驗解決，而不是靠辯論。
    • You're willing to learn to code. Most of what we do runs on MATLAB or Python. Nobody arrives knowing it; everybody leaves knowing it. 你願意學寫程式。我們的工作多以 MATLAB 或 Python 進行。沒有人是一開始就會的，但每個人離開時都會。
    • You want your work to reach a patient. Our findings run from a laboratory task to a randomised clinical trial, often within the same project. 你希望研究能真的幫到病人。我們的發現常在同一個計畫中，從實驗室作業一路走到隨機臨床試驗。

    Write to us — tell us which of the four experiments above you found most interesting, and why. 寫信給我們——告訴我們上面四個實驗中你覺得哪一個最有趣，以及為什麼。
